## Start a capture

Press <kbd>Print Screen</kbd>. All your monitors freeze instantly and dim a little, so you can pick the part you want.

You can also start a capture:

- With a **click** on the tray icon.
- From the tray icon's menu (right-click), with **Capturar** (Capture).
- By opening Beauty Screenshot again from the Start menu while it's already running.

If you prefer another shortcut, change it in [Settings](/en/screenshot/docs/settings/). While Beauty Screenshot is running, the Windows Snipping Tool doesn't respond to that shortcut: Beauty Screenshot takes it.

## Select

- **Drag** to mark the part you want. The selection has rounded corners, just like the final image.
- A **click without dragging** takes the whole monitor.
- **Drag from inside** the selection to move it.
- **Right-click** clears the selection so you can start over. With no selection, it cancels the capture.

## Copy or save

When you let go, a bar appears with **Copiar** (Copy) and **Guardar** (Save). You can also use the keyboard:

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> or double-click the selection | What you chose in Settings: **Copy**, unless you changed it |
| <kbd>Ctrl+C</kbd> | Copy |
| <kbd>Ctrl+S</kbd> | Save |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Cancel |

- **Copy** puts the image on the clipboard, ready to paste. The transparent corners are kept in apps that accept PNG.
- **Save** opens the dialog to choose where and under what name. If you turn on **Guardar sin preguntar** (Save without asking) in Settings, it goes straight to your screenshots folder. It always saves as PNG.

To do both at once, turn on **Copiar también al guardar** (Also copy when saving).

## After capturing

The capture flies to the bottom-right corner and rests there for a moment, with a label saying whether it was copied, saved or both.

- Take several in a row and they **stack up**.
- With the mouse over it, the stack **stays put**.
- A **click** on a saved capture opens its folder, with the file selected.

If you turn off **Mostrar notificaciones** (Show notifications), the capture leaves right away.

## The tray icon

Beauty Screenshot lives in the system tray, next to the clock. Right-click its icon for:

- **Capturar** (Capture)
- **Abrir carpeta de capturas** (Open screenshots folder)
- **Ajustes…** (Settings)
- **Iniciar con Windows** (Start with Windows)
- **Salir** (Quit)

If you don't see the icon, it may be hidden under the **^** arrow in the tray. Drag it out so it's always visible.

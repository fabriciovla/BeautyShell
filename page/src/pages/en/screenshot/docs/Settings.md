Open Settings by right-clicking the tray icon → **Ajustes…**. Changes apply instantly.

## Captura (Capture)

| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Atajo de teclado** (Keyboard shortcut) | The combination that starts a capture. Click the button and press the new one; <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels. | <kbd>Print Screen</kbd> |
| **Enter y doble clic** (Enter and double-click) | What they do when you confirm without the bar: **Copiar** (Copy) or **Guardar** (Save). | Copy |

On their own, without <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>, <kbd>Alt</kbd>, <kbd>Shift</kbd> or <kbd>Win</kbd>, you can only use <kbd>Print Screen</kbd>, <kbd>Pause</kbd>, <kbd>Scroll Lock</kbd> and <kbd>F1</kbd> through <kbd>F24</kbd>. Any other key would get in the way of typing, so it needs a modifier.

## Guardado (Saving)

| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Carpeta** (Folder) | Where screenshots are saved. | Pictures |
| **Guardar sin preguntar** (Save without asking) | Saves straight to the folder, without the dialog. | Off |
| **Nombre de archivo** (File name) | What screenshots are called. An example shows below it. | `Captura {fecha} {hora}` |
| **Copiar también al guardar** (Also copy when saving) | Besides the file, puts the image on the clipboard. | Off |

In the name you can use:

| Tag | Replaced with | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{fecha}` | The date | `2026-10-27` |
| `{hora}` | The time | `14.05.09` |
| `{n}` | A number that goes up with each capture | `001` |

If a file with that name already exists, it isn't overwritten: a number is added, like `(2)`.

## Apariencia (Appearance)

| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Esquinas redondeadas** (Rounded corners) | The corner radius of the selection and the image, from 0 (square) to 24 px. The corners are transparent. | 10 px |
| **Oscurecido** (Dimming) | How dark the screen gets while you select, from 0 to 85%. | 50% |
| **Mostrar ayuda** (Show hint) | A short hint at the bottom of the screen when a capture starts. | On |
| **Mostrar notificaciones** (Show notifications) | The thumbnail with its label in the corner after copying or saving. | On |
| **Sonido al capturar** (Capture sound) | A soft camera sound when copying or saving. | On |

## General

| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Iniciar con Windows** (Start with Windows) | Keeps it ready in the tray when your PC starts. It's also in the tray icon's menu. | What you chose when installing |
